Tulipa lending pool on Ethereum enables you to earn passive yield on your USDC without liquidation risk, impermanent loss, or lockups. Your deposits generate returns from borrower interest as they deploy capital across Gearbox strategies using stablecoin-correlated assets like [Convex Staked LP](https://curve.convexfinance.com/stake), [Resolv USR](https://resolv.xyz/) and [Pendle PT tokens](https://app.pendle.finance) as collateral.

Borrowers leverage correlated stables against stable-denominated assets which minimizes directional volatility and creates predictable fixed-income strategies. Withdrawals are available as long as utilization is not 100% and your yield automatically compounds. Review the "Collaterals and their rates" tab to see borrower collateral options and risk parameters.
