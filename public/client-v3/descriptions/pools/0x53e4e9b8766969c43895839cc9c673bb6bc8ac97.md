EdgeUltra Yield lending pool on Plasma enables you to earn passive yield on your USDT0 without liquidation risk, impermanent loss, or lockups. Your deposits generate returns from borrower interest as they deploy capital across Gearbox strategies using stablecoin-correlated assets like [Pendle PT tokens](https://app.pendle.finance), [Ethena USDe](https://ethena.fi), [syrupUSDT](https://www.maple.finance), and [Curve LP tokens](https://curve.finance) as collateral.

Borrowers leverage correlated stables against stable-denominated assets which minimizes directional volatility and creates predictable fixed-income strategies for Pendle PT positions. Withdrawals are available as long as utilization is not 100% and your yield automatically compounds. Review the "Collaterals and their rates" tab to see borrower collateral options and risk parameters.
