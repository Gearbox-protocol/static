[kpk](https://kpk.io) brings over five years of institutional asset management expertise—trusted by the Ethereum Foundation, Aave, and Lido—applying professional risk frameworks and continuous monitoring to this permissionless lending market. Withdrawals are available as long as utilization is not 100% and your yield automatically compounds.
