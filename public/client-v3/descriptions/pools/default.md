Gearbox Lending pools enable you to earn passive yield without liquidation risk, impermanent loss, or lockups. Your deposits generate returns from borrower interest as they deploy capital across Gearbox strategies. Withdrawals are available as long as utilization is not 100% and your yield automatically compounds and increase in value over time. Review the "Collaterals and their rates" tab to see borrower collateral options and risk parameters. [Learn more](https://docs.gearbox.finance/lending-market/pools-and-apy)
