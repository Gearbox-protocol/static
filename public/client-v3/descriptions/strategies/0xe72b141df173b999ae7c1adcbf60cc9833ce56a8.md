Deposit ETH collateral to borrow and multiply your position in [ETH+](https://app.reserve.org/ethereum/token/0xe72b141df173b999ae7c1adcbf60cc9833ce56a8/overview), a diversified liquid staking basket that earns yields across multiple protocols. Both your collateral and borrowed funds deploy together, amplifying staking returns.

Primary risk is ETH+ depegging, but dual oracles protect against price manipulation and Reserve Protocol stakers provide first-loss insurance to absorb withdrawal liquidity issues. [ETHPlus details](https://app.reserve.org/ethereum/token/0xe72b141df173b999ae7c1adcbf60cc9833ce56a8/overview).
